Shadow Warrior (Shareware)
==========================

The shareware episode of Shadow Warrior (3D Realms, 1997) - Lo Wang against the Zilla Enterprises' demons -
played through JFSW, Jonathon Fowler's port of the original source. The v1.2 shareware data is included. To
play the full game, put your own sw.grp (the registered version) in this folder in place of the shareware one.


Controls
--------

D-Pad / left stick   Move forward and back, turn
Right stick          Strafe, look up and down (on a pad that has one)
Square               Fire
Cross                Crouch (double press: toggle run mode)
Circle               Open, activate
Triangle             Jump
L1                   Next inventory item
R1                   Next weapon
L2 / R2              Strafe left / right
Select               Use the inventory item
Start                Menu (double press: map)

To leave the game: press Reset on the console, or hold Start + Select (the console, the Pi and the PC
stick). On Windows: Start for the menu, then Quit.

The settings (sw.cfg) and the saved games are kept in this folder.
